> [!garden] Description
> Intelligent Agents is a [unit](https://www.bristol.ac.uk/unit-programme-catalogue/UnitDetails.jsa?ayrCode=26%2F27\&unitCode=COMS30094) in my course at University of Bristol. This page acts as a main hub for notes related to content from this unit.
>
> The unit aims to teach foundations in agentic theory, multi-agent systems and relevant game theory ideas.
