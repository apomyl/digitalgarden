> [!definition] Regularisation Parameter $\alpha$ or $\lambda$
> The [[Hyperparameter Tuning|Hyperparameter]] that controls how heavily large weights are penalised in parameter norm penalty [[Regularisation Techniques]].
>
> $\alpha \in [0, \infty)$
> Setting $\alpha$ to 0 results in no regularization. Larger values of α correspond to more regularisation.
