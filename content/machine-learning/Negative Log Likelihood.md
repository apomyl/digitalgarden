> [!definition] Negative Log Likelihood
> A standard [[Cost Functions|Loss function]] to measure how well a model's predicted probabilities match up to true class.
>
> $E(\mathbf{w}) = -\ln p(\mathbf{t}\vert{}\mathbf{w})$
> (side note: probability of target given weights)
