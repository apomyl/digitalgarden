> [!definition] Gaussian Distribution
> A continuous probability distribution (shaped like a bell curve).
> $X \sim \mathcal{N}(\mu, \sigma^2)$
> where $\mu$ is the mean and $\sigma^2$ is the variance and $\sigma$ the standard deviation.
>
> ![[Images/Gaussian Distribution-1790173866724.webp|256x168]]

[[Probability Density Function]] expressed as:
$f(x) = \frac{1}{\sigma \sqrt{2\pi}} \exp\left(-\frac{(x - \mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right)$
