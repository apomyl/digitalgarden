> [!definition] Probabilistic
> Model assumes that the relationship is uncertain and outputs a probability distribution over possible outcomes

> [!definition] Deterministic
> Model assumes that given a specific input, the system will always produce the same single output.
>
> e.g. $y = a_0 + a_1 x$
