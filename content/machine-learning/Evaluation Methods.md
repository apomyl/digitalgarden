## Key Terms

- True Positive - $TP$
- True Negative - $TN$
- False Positive - $FP$
- False Negative - $FN$
- Total Positive - $P$
- Total Negative - $N$

> [!definition] Accuracy
> Proportion of all predictions model got correct.
> $\frac{TP+TN}{P+N}$

> [!definition] Sensitivity or Recall or True Positive Rate
> Proportion of correct positive predictions.
> $\frac{TP}{P}$

> [!definition] Specificity or True Negative Rate
> Proportion of correct negative predictions.
> $\frac{TN}{N}$

> [!definition] Precision
> Proportion of predictions model claimed were positive that were correct.
> $\frac{TP}{TP+FP}$
