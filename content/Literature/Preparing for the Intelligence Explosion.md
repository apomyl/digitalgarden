>[!garden] Summary
>Paper showcases how AI has progressed and how it might progress in the future, highlighting the rate at which AI will speed up research, labelled the "Intelligence Explosion".
>
>The authors lay out some "grand challenges" that need to be considered in the process of achieving AGI preparedness. Also discussing how we can address these challenges and why we should address them instead of deferring them to be later solved by superintelligence.
