>[!garden] Summary
>Paper provides definitions for different norm types and breaks down a normative approach to governance in sociotechnical systems.
>
>A normative approach focuses on establishing social agency, governing the [[Multi-Agent Systems|MAS]] through social norms, which define how an agent should act within the society. However, agents maintain their autonomy and can choose to violate norms based on their own internal beliefs at the cost of a sanction or punishment.
