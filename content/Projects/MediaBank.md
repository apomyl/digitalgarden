>[!garden] MediaBank - Software Engineering Project
>As part of the Software Engineering Project in my 2nd year at University, my 3 teammates and I worked for a local company: [Floating Harbour Film Studio](https://floatingharbour.co.uk/?utm_source=GBP&utm_medium=organic&utm_campaign=video-production) alongside their partners at [WECA](https://www.westofengland-ca.gov.uk). We developed a media storage application for internal use.

This was a full stack development with a focus on the process by implementing AGILE processes and building communication skills within our team and with our client.

Tech Stack:

- Python Backend
