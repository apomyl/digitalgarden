> [!definition] Chain Rule
> Technique for finding the derivative of a composite function (brackets).
> $F'(x) = f'(g(x)) \cdot g'(x)$
> $\frac{dy}{dx} = \frac{dy}{du} \cdot \frac{du}{dx}$

> [!eg] Example
> $f(x) = (3x + 5)^4$
> $F`(x) = 3 \cdot 4(3x+4)^3$
> $F`(x) = 12(3x+4)^3$
