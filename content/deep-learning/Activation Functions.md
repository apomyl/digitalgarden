> [!definition] Activation Function
> A non-linear function that determines the output of a unit in a [[Neural Networks|Neural Network]] based on a weighted sum of its inputs and biases.

> [!eg] Types
>
> - [[Sigmoid]]
> - [[Rectifying Linear Unit|ReLU]]
> - [[Leaky ReLU]]
