> [!definition]
> Problem arising in deep [[Neural Networks]] from the distribution of each layer's inputs changes during training, as the parameters of the previous layers change. These changes propagate through the network, complicating the training as each layer has to adapt to constantly shifting inputs.
>
> "The change in the distribution of network activations due to the change in network parameters during training".
>
> Solved with [[Batch Normalisation]]
