> [!definition]
> [[Regularisation Techniques|Regularisation]] technique of adding random noise to data (or in other areas) so that the model doesn't [[Underfitting vs Overfitting|overfit]] to the noiseless data set.
>
> This can also be applied to:
>
> - weights
> - activations
> - hidden layers (e.g. [[Dropout]])
>
> By adding noise to all stages, the model becomes more fault-tolerant, and has to adapt.
