> [!definition] MSE
> Average between real and predicted valued
> $\text{MSE} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (y_i - \hat{y}_i)^2$
>
> [[Cost Functions|Cost function]] using Empirical Risk MSE - gradient
> $J(\mathbf{X}; \mathbf{w}) = \frac{1}{2\vert{}\mathbf{X}\vert{}} \sum_{\mathbf{x} \in \mathbf{X}} \left(\mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{x} - f^*(\mathbf{x})\right)^2$
