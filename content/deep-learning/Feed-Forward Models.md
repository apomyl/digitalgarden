> [!definition] Feed Forward Neural Networks
> Machine learning models that take in input and produce an output.
> Data flows through [[Neural Networks|multiple layers]] in a single direction without feeding back.
>
> Layers are fully connected, meaning every neuron is connected to every neuron in the layer before and after.
