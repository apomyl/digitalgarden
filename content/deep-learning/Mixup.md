> [!definition]
> [[Data Augmentation]] technique where you mix two images together with random variable $\lambda \in [0,1]$ $\hat{x}=\lambda x_{i}+(1-\lambda)x_{j}$
> The target becomes a soft label vector (a weighted combination of both classes) instead of a one-hot encoded vector.
