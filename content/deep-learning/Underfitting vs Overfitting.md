> [!definition] Underfitting
> A problem arising when a machine learning model is too simple, does not represent training data well and doesn't perform well on testing data.

> [!definition] Overfitting
> A problem arising when a machine learning model is too complex, fits training data perfectly but doesn't perform well on new test data.

![[Images/Underfitting vs Overfitting-1790773823954.webp]]

> [!success] Solutions to Overfitting
>
> - [[Cross-validation]]
> - [[L2 Regularisation]]
> - [[Dropout]]
> - Collect more data
