> [!definition]
> Metric for distance between two points
>
> For two points $\boldsymbol{u}, \boldsymbol{v} \in \mathbb{R}^n$:
>
> $d_1(\boldsymbol{u}, \boldsymbol{v}) = \Vert{}\boldsymbol{u} - \boldsymbol{v}\Vert{}_1 = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \vert{}u_i - v_i\vert{}$
>
> (or as the distance of a weight vector $\boldsymbol{w}$ from the origin: $\Vert{}\boldsymbol{w}\Vert{}_1 = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \vert{}w_i\vert{}$
