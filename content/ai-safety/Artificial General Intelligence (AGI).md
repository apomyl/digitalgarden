> [!definition] Definitions
> General idea: Artifical intelligence that can solve intellectual tasks across fields at a level equal to or greater than a human being.
>
> "Expert AGI" (Morris et al.): performance exceeding “at least 90th percentile of skilled adults” across a “wide range of non-physical tasks, including metacognitive tasks like learning new skills”
