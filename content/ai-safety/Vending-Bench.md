> [!definition]
> A method to measure AI models' ability to autonomously do economically valuable work.
>
> Vending-Bench is a simulation where AI models run a vending machine business over a year and are scored on their bank account balance at the end. They start with:
>
> - \$500
> - An email inbox
> - Internet search
> - A bank account
> - Business tools like spreadsheets and ad platforms
