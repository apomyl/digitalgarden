> [!definition] Definition
> [[Agents]] choose well given the information and resources available at the time.

The simple rational-choice expression:
$a_t \in \text{argmax}_{a \in A} \mathbb{E}[P | h_t, a]$
where:

- A is the set of available actions
- P denotes the performance measure
- $h_t$ the history of observations and actions available to the agent up to time t
- $\mathbb{E}$ reflects uncertainty about future outcomes
- $\text{argmax}$ selects the action(s) with the highest expected performance

> [!warning] A rational action can still have a bad outcome
> Rational behaviour can only be discusses relative to what counts as a good outcome
