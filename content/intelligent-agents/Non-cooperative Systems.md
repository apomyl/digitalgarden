> [!definition]
> A [[Multi-Agent Systems|multi-agent system]] where each agent has its own desires and preferences, which may conflict with those of other agents.
>
> Agents now need to be _incentivised_ to choose a desirable plan of action.

We use [[Game Theory in Agent Systems|game theory]] as the foundation for solving this problem.
