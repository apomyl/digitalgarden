> [!definition]
> A measure of collective efficiency for [[Policy|policies]].

Joint policy $\pi$ is **Pareto-dominated** by $\pi'$ if
$\forall i : U_i(\pi') \ge U_i(\pi) \quad \text{and} \quad \exists i : U_i(\pi') > U_i(\pi)$
_(every single agent is atleast as well off + at least one agent strictly better off)_

$\pi$ is **Pareto-optimal** if no such $\pi'$ exists

> [!success] Why is it useful?
>
> - minimum standard of efficiency
> - judging/choosing [[Nash Equilibrium|equilibria]]
> - in cooperative settings with a common reward, the pareto-optimal policy is the best one
