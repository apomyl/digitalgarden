> [!definition]
> Solution to balancing [[Practical Reasoning]] deliberation and reaction by splitting agents operation into 3 layers running at the same time at different speeds:

| Layer                  |    Timescale    | Function                                          | Mechanism                                                |
| :--------------------- | :-------------: | :------------------------------------------------ | :------------------------------------------------------- |
| Deliberative           | Hours / Minutes | Strategic goal selection & long-term planning     | Heavy search / [[Belief-Desire-Intention\|BDI]] planning |
| Execution / Monitoring |     Seconds     | Checking intention validity & reconsidering plans | Goal-progress checks                                     |
| Reactive               |  Milliseconds   | Collision avoidance & emergency override          | Reflexive condition-action rules                         |
