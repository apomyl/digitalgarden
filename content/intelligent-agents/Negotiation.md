> [!definition]
> A communication process in which [[Agents|agents]] exchange offers and counter-offers to reach an agreement on matters of common interest.

> [!tldr] Core Concepts
>
> - preferences over outcomes
>   - they have distinct orderings or numerical utility functions $u_i(\omega)$ over the set of possible agreements $\omega \in \Omega$.
> - reservation value / walk-away point
>   - the minimum utility an agent must receive to accept an offer
>   - if an offer gives less utility than the reservation value $r_i$, the agent walks away with no deal
> - concession strategy
>   - the tactic or function an agent uses to decide how much utility to surrender across rounds of counter-offers over time
>   - [[Tit-for-Tat Strategy]]
> - agreement creates commitments
>   - once both agents mutually accept an offer, the deal becomes a [[Commitment-Based Communication|commitment]]
