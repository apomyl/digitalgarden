> [!definition]
> The exchange of information and acts among agents in [[Multi-Agent Systems]]. Communication is essential to distributed agent systems but does not by itself ensure [[Coordination in Agent Systems|coordination]].

Communication can be studied at different levels:

- **Meaning and acts** - [[Speech Acts]], [[Communicative Acts]]
- **Protocols and languages** - [[Agent Communication Languages]]
- **Social interactions** - [[Negotiation]], [[Argumentation]], [[Commitment-Based Communication]], [[Trust and Reputation]]

Communication is important to a [[Normative Multi-Agent Systems|normative]] approach to agent systems.
