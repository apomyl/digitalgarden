> [!definition] Definition
> **Reactive [[Agents]]** - acts based on current observation alone
> $a_t= \pi(o_t)$
>
> - e.g. obstacle ahead -> stop
>
> Things to remember:
>
> - not proactive
> - no memory
> - no anticipation
> - implicitly goal-directed (serve a purpose without knowing it)
> - fast, simple and tightly coupled to the environment
