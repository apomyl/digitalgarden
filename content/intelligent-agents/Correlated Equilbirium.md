> [!definition]
> An alternative [[Policy]] to [[Nash Equilibrium]] where agents are given private recommended actions by a shared signal.
>
> Agents choose actions from a probability distribution over joint actions. This is decided by designer ahead of time. Each agent is privately told its own part of the joint action (recommended action).
>
> Equilibrium is defined as no player having incentive to disobey their recommendation.

Example: cars at a traffic light

- they can both either Go or Stop
- in a Nash Equilibrium they will both randomly choose an action, if both Go they will crash, if both Stop they will wait indefinitely
- in a Correlated Equilibrium, our shared signal is a traffic light that proposes recommended actions (Stop, Go) or (Go, Stop) with 50% each, so that atleast one car goes through safely
