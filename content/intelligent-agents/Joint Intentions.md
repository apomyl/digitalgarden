> [!definition]
> When designing [[Multi-Agent Systems|MAS]], computing [[Task Allocation]] for a [[Joint Action and Policy|Joint Action]] isn't the end of the work, in the real world there are changing factors and [[Coordination in Agent Systems|coordination]] could still fail over time.
>
> We need to define joint intentions like in [[Belief-Desire-Intention]] agents, this would include defining rules for:
>
> - commitment to joint goal
> - mutual awareness of status
> - when to communicate failure
> - when to reallocate tasks
