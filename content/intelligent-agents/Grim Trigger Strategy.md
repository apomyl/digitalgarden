> [!definition]
> A [[Policy]] to incentivise [[Cooperation in Agent Systems]] in infinitely/indefinitely repeating games.
>
> ```
> Cooperate in round 1
> Keep cooperating as long as both agents have always cooperated
> If someone defects, defect forever after
> ```

> [!question] When does it actually work?
> In an infinitely repeated [[Prisoner's Dilemma]], Grim Trigger sustains mutual cooperation as a [[Subgame Perfect Equilibrium]] (SPE) if and only if agents are [[Patience|patient]] enough ($\gamma \ge \frac{T - R}{T - P}$), making the infinite punishment worse than the one-off temptation to defect.
>
> For the example prisoner's dilemma:
> $\gamma \ge \frac{0 - (-1)}{0 - (-3)} = \frac{1}{3}$
