> [!definition]
> In [[Multi-agent Reinforcement Learning]], from any one agent's view, an action's payoff can change as the other agents' policies shift.
>
> Formally: the process agent $i$ experiences is stationary only if the whole joint policy is fixed – but learning means $\pi^z$ keeps changing for everyone at once.
>
> This can cause cyclic learning dynamic - agents chase each other's shifting policies without ever settling down.
