> [!garden] Game theory for Agents
> The study of how [[Multi-Agent Systems|agents]] make decisions when their success depends on the choices of others.
>
> _"Game theory is the field of applied mathematics that **models interactions between multiple agents** as "games" of strategy between "players". It is a scientific method of **describing, predicting, evaluating, and selecting choices** (or "actions"), **often with the view of maximising utility outcomes** (or "payoffs") for one or more players."_

> [!bug] Cooperative vs [[Non-cooperative Systems]]
>
> - fully [[Cooperation in Agent Systems|cooperate]] in order to achieve shared goal
> - each agent has its own desires and preferences, which may conflict with those of other agents
>
> We need game theory in order to build non-cooperative systems that are more complex than full cooperation.
