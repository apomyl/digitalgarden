>[!definition]
>The administration of a system, through structures, rules and other enforcement mechanics, by its participants to regulate interactions while maintaining the participants' autonomy.
>
>In [[Sociotechnical Systems]], stakeholders are all participants who are [[Autonomous Agents|autonomous]] peers, none has authority over the others.

>[!question] What challenges does governance aim to solve?
>- How can we accommodate continually changing stakeholder needs?
>- How can multiple stakeholders collaborate in a sustainable, efficient manner?
>- How can individual ownership and control be respected as autonomous parties interoperate?
>- How can resources be added or dropped dynamically at runtime?
>- How can coalitions be constructed and enacted to dynamically share resources while entertaining challenges such the stakeholders’ needs changing unexpectedly?
