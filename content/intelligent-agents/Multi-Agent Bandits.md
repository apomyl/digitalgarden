> [!definition]
> Several [[Reinforcement Learning]]/[[Multi-Armed Bandit]] agents share a [[Repeated Normal-Form Games|repeated normal-form game]], but nobody knows the [[Reward Functions|reward]] matrix in advance – each only observes its own reward for the joint action played.

> [!definition] Non-Stationarity
> From any one agent's view, an action's payoff can change over time as opponents' [[Policy|policies]] shift.
