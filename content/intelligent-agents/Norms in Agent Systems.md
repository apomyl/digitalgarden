> [!definition]
> A norm characterises "sound" or "normal" interaction among members of a social group, reflecting their mutual expectations.
>
> Formally for [[Sociotechnical Systems]]:
>
> A norm is a **directed normative relationship** between participants in the context of an organisation.
>
> - directed - from one agent to another
> - normative - agents can choose to violate norm
> - relationship - dynamic social bond, mutual state tracking

Norms can be violated at the risk of consequences like sanctions, reputation loss, etc. Norms act as a basis for [[Accountability in Agent Systems|accountability]].

Norms provide a rigorous basis for coherence, which we view as a relaxed notion of correctness that accommodates restoring a “good” state after a violation.
